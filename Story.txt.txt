My first day of college was a mixture of excitement, nervousness, and curiosity. I woke up earlier than usual, even though I had barely slept the night before. I stood in front of my mirror, wondering if I looked like a real college student. After getting ready, I packed my bag and left home with a big smile and a few butterflies in my stomach.

When I reached the college campus, I was amazed by how large and lively it was. Students were walking in every direction, talking with their friends, checking their schedules, and searching for their classrooms. For a moment, I felt completely lost. I looked at my timetable several times before finally finding my first classroom.

As I entered, I noticed that most students were strangers to one another. I quietly chose a seat near the window. Soon, a student sitting beside me introduced herself. Her friendly smile immediately made me feel comfortable. We started talking about our classes, where we were from, and how nervous we both were. By the time the professor arrived, I already felt less alone.

The first lecture was interesting, but I realized that college was very different from school. The professor expected us to participate, think independently, and take responsibility for our work. During lunch, my new friend and I explored the cafeteria and met a few more classmates. We laughed about how confused we had been that morning.

When I finally returned home in the evening, I felt tired but happy. My first day had not been perfect, but it had been memorable. I had stepped into a completely new world and made my first friend. As I went to sleep that night, I knew that my college journey had begun, and I was excited to discover what the future would bring.

I came home from a wonderful day at school. My mom tells me I have to transfer.
